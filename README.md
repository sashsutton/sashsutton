## About Me

I hold a BSc in Mathematics & Computer Science from Aix-Marseille University and a BA in Music Production & Sound Engineering from Middlesex University. Most of my work sits at the intersection of ML theory and applied engineering, building things that require understanding the maths underneath, not just calling an API.

The music and the engineering have never been separate for me. I produce electronic music, play guitar, and at some point built a computer vision instrument that turns hand movements into sound.

## Projects

### [Koda Marketplace](https://github.com/koda-lab/koda-web-app)
Live: [kodas.works](https://www.kodas.works/en)
> **Next.js 15 · TypeScript · Stripe Connect · AWS S3 · Pusher · MongoDB**

A marketplace for no-code automations, shipped with a team. I built the financial layer: Stripe Connect with an 85/15 commission split and admin-controlled refund reversals, real-time buyer/seller messaging via Pusher, and secure file delivery through private S3 buckets with signed URLs. Available in English, French, Spanish, and German.

---

### [Air Harp](https://github.com/sashsutton/computer_vision_air_harp)
> **Python · OpenCV · MediaPipe · Pygame**

A computer vision instrument. Point your index finger at a webcam and play a harp by crossing detection zones on screen. Built with MediaPipe for hand landmark tracking and OpenCV for real-time rendering. Started as a question: what happens when computer vision meets music.

---

### [Neural Vision 3D](https://github.com/sashsutton/neural-network-numbers)
Live: [neural-network-numbers.vercel.app](https://neural-network-numbers.vercel.app)
> **React · Three.js · Python · NumPy**

An interactive 3D visualisation of a neural network that recognises hand-drawn digits. The inference engine is written from scratch in Python with NumPy (no ML frameworks), with a 784→512→256→128→11 architecture. The frontend renders live neuron activations using React Three Fiber.

---

### [Vector Search Engine](https://github.com/sashsutton/vector-search-engine)
> **Python · FastAPI · NumPy · Sentence-Transformers (BERT)**

A semantic search engine built from scratch. Documents are ranked by meaning using cosine similarity over 384-dimensional BERT embeddings, served through FastAPI. Deployed as a microservices architecture across Render and Vercel.

---

### [ML Library](https://github.com/sashsutton/ML_library)
> **C · CMake**

A neural network library in pure C: matrix operations, ReLU and Sigmoid activations, dense layer forward propagation, MSE loss. No dependencies. Built to understand what is actually happening below the abstractions.

---

### [WARC NLP Pipeline](https://github.com/sashsutton/warc-nlp-pipeline)
> **Python · Jupyter · Pandas · Trafilatura · SolR Wayback · BERTopic · CamemBERT · LDA**

NLP research on web archives at LIS (Laboratoire d'Informatique et Systèmes, Luminy). Extracts and processes WARC files from the French National Library's archive of Marsactu (a Marseille-based investigative outlet) into structured DataFrames, then applies topic modelling (LDA, BERTopic) and CamemBERT-based analysis to track thematic evolution over time.
